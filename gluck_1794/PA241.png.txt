(Nod. 21.) Grahmata. (Nod. 21. 22.) 241
1. Un ja kas kahdu grehku darrijs, zaur ko winſch to nahwi irr pelnijs, un tas nokaujams irr, un tu winnu pee kahdu kohku pakahrſi.
2. Tad laid winna nomirruſi meefa pee ta kohka pahr nakti ne paleek, bet aprohz winnu taſ deenâ, jo weens pa- kahrtais irr preekſch Deewa weens labſts, tà ne buhs tew tawu ſemmi ap- gahnibt, ko tas Kungs taws Deews tew par ihpaſchumu dohd. Gal. 3, 13.
Ta 22. Nodalla.
1. Ta tuwata ſohpeem ſapalihoſ: Seewai ne buhs wihriſkas drehbes, neds wihreem ſee- wiſkas ueſt, 5. Kas ar atraſtu putnu ligſdu ja darra, 6. Kâ buhs nammus ustraiſiht, 8. Un wihna-dahrſus dehſtiht, 9. Kâ to buhs ſohriht, kas no ſawas ſeewas niknu ſlawu zeſ, 13. Ta laulibas-pahrkahpſchanas ſoh- diba, 22. Kad kahda jumprawa tohp ar war- ru apſmeeta, 23. 25. 28.
2. Tew ne buhs tawa brahla wehrſi jeb awi redſeht allojam, un no winna atrautees, tew buhs tohs tawam brah- lam atkal peeweſt. 2 Mohſ. gr. 23, 4.
3. Un ja taws brahlis pee tew tuwu ne irr, jeb tu winnu ne vaſihſti, tad buhs tew tohs tawa nammâ fadſiht, ko tee pee tew irr, teekams taws brahlis tohs mekle, tad buhs tew winnam tohs atdoht.
4. Tà patt buhs tawa brahla ehſeli, jeb winna wehrſi redſeht us zelku pakrit- tufchu, un no teem atrautees, bet uszell winnu tikkuſchi. Matt. 12. 11. Luhk. 14, 5.
5. Wihra drehbes ne buhs kahdai fee- wai waſkaht, un wihram ne buhs fee- wu drehbes apwilkt, jo wiſs, kas ſcho darra, irr tam Kunga tawam Dee- wam weena negantiba.
6. Kad tu putna-liaſdu uselj us zelku, jeb kahdâ kohkâ, jeb wihras ſemmes ar behrneem, jeb pauteem, un ta mahtite pahr teem behrneem, jeb pauteem ſehſch:
7. Tad tew ne buhs to mahtiti lihds ar teem behrneem nemt, palaid to mahtiti, un tohs behrnus nemm preekſch tewim, ka tew labbi klahjahs, un tu ilgi dſihwo.
8. Kad tu jaunu nammu uszehrti, tad buhs tew kahdu peeſleenamu (lehni) us tawu jumtu darriht, ka tu ne kahdu affins.
