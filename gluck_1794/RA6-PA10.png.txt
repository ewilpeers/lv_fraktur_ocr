Jahn. 21, 24. Un winnu Wahrdi ne irr tuwami par Zilweku Wahrdeem, biſſitt kà tas teefcham irr, par Deewa Wahrdeem. 1 Teſſ. 2, 13.
Bet eekſch ta Jauna Teſtamenta dafchadas Grahmatas irr et raſtinas; Tahs pirmas tſchetras Grahmatas tohp fauktas Ewangeliums. No tahm to pirmu Matteïs, to ohtru Markus, to trefchu Luhkas, to zettunu Jahnis usrakſtijis.
Ewangeliums irr weens greekifchkis Wahrds, un irr tik daus kà weena preeziga Wehſts. Kà nu weenam pee Nahwes paſuddinatam Ewangeliums neekam nekahda Wehſts tik preeziga warr buht, kà ween ſchi, ka tam buht kà woht. Tà tohy ta Mahziba, eekſch ka wiſſeem pee Nahwes paſuddinatam Ewangeliums, tas irr ta preeziga Wehſts, jeb Schehlaſtibas Mahziba dehweta. Scho Grahmatu Uſrakſtitaji, tohy Preezas-Mahzibaſſeja, tapehz, ka tee to preezigu Wehſti tai Pafaulei usrakſtijſchi, ka ta Pafaules Dahrſa dohta Apfohlifchana peepildita, tahs Seewas Sehkla Kriſtusdſimmis, un wiſſu darrijs un zeetis, ko winkam par muhfu Peſtifchanu un Swehtibu bij zeeſt un darriht.
Pehz tahm to Apuſtulu Darbi laſſami. Apuſtuls irr arri weens greekifchkis Wahrds, un irr tik daus kà weens Wehſtneffis jeb Suhtihis. Kà muhfu Kungs Kriſtus fawu Mahzibas Ammatu eefahze, israudſija winſch kà padefmits Wihrus, tohs winſch Apuſtulus noſauze, Luhk. 6, 13. Kas Deus Muhſchu apkahrt winna bija, winna Mahzibu dſirdeja, winna Brihnuma-Deus redſeja, ka tee tai Pafaulei tik labbi no ſcheem wiſſeem, kà arri no winna Deusſchamzelfchanas warretu Leezibu doht. Luhk. 24, 48. Ap. Darb. 1, 22. Rod. 3, 12.
Tee pafchi isgahje vehz ta Kunga Kriſtus Usbraukſchanas pa wiſſu Pafaulei tſo, kad tas Swehts Gars pahr teem wiſſeem redſobt bija isleets, Ap. Darb. 2, 1, 12.

Kà nu teem vafcheem pee ſchihs Mahzibas irr klahjees, un ko tee krah teem, wiſs wairak Pahwiſam tam Pagann-Mahzitajam irr darrijſchi, un tenau tas Wels un nikni Laudis, to Deewa Darbu un ſcho Mahzibu ne wanſſe aiskaweht, tas tohp eekſch to Apuſtulu Darbu Grahmatas aprakſtihts.
To Apuſtulu leelaka Darboſchana bija, ka ta Mahziba, ko tee paſchiflo dinaja, arri pee tahm Draudſehm paturreta taptu; tapehz ſtiprinaja tee jata buhdami tohs Tizzigus ar Grahmatahm, no kurreem Pahwils, kas wiſs nardſtrahdajs, 1 Kor. 15, 10. jo wairak rakſtijs, kas pehz us ſcho Wihſi, ka tahs ſcho Grahmatâ ſtahw, kohpâ farakſtitas irr.
Ta pehdeja Grahmata irr weena Parahdiſchana, kas Jahnam tam Apuſtulam notikkuſi, eekſch tumſchahm Praweeſchu Bildehn un Wahrdeem winnam rahdihts kluïs, kà ta Kunga Kriſtus Draudſei libdiſſe faules Gallam klahfees, kà ta no Kriſtus Mahzibas atkahpuſi Draudſi un Muhki jeb Bahbele to ihſtenu Draudſi wajahs un ſpeedihs, kamehr Deus zelfees un Bahbeli nofohdihs.
Tas irr, Mihkais Laweeti! kas man tew no ſchihs Grahmatas bij jata Zellees nu, tohpi gaifch, jo taws Gaifchums naht, un ta Kunga Gohduslezz pahr tew, Eſa. 60, 1. ne paleezi ilgaki eekſch tahs pirmajas Neſſunus. Pateizi Deewam no Sirds, ka winſch arri tew fawu Wahrdu tawa Wahrdeewa dewis laſſiht; Luhdſ winnu par winna Swehtu Garru, kas tew apgaifme, kà wiſſe tam lehnigam Deewam par winna Schehlaſtibu no Sirds pateiz. Deews lai tew dohd fawu Schehlaſtibu zaur JEſu Kriſtu muhfu Kungas. Amen! Riga, 4tâ deenâ Lappu Mehn. 1685.
