darrihts, 1. Tas Salihdſinaſchanas-wahks, 6.

ſchanas-altaris, 25. Ta kwehta ſwaidiſchanas-

1. Ta darrija Bezaleëlû to ſchkirſtu no
Sittim-kohka, pufſtrefchu ohlekſchu
winna garrums, un pufs ohtru ohlek-
ſchu winna plattums, un pufſohtru
ohlekſchu winna augſtums.

ſeltu, no eekſchas, un no ahra puffes, un
winſch darrija tam weenu krohni ap-

rinkus, pee winna tſchetreem ſtuhreem,

bija, un diwi rinki winna ohtrâ puffè.
4. Un winſch darrija neſſamas kahrtis
no Sittim-kohka un pahrwilke tahs ar
ſeltu.

kahrtis rinkôs, pee ta ſchkirſta fahneem,
ka to ſchkirſtu neſt warretu.

ſchanas-wahku no ſchkihſta ſeltu, pufs
trefchu ohlekſchu bija winna garrums,
un pufs ohtru ohlekſchu winna plat-
tums.
