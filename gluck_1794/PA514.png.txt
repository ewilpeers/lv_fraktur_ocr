8. Bet tas Kungs fazzija us mannu tehwn Dahwidu: Tapehz ka tu tawa firdi effi apnehmees mannam wahrdam weenu nammu darriht, effi tu gaun labbi darrijs, ka tas tawa firdi bijis irr.
9. Tomehr Tew ne buhs to nammu pataifiht, bet taws dehls, kas no ta-weem gurneem nahks, tas ustaifihs mannam wahrdam to nammu.
10. Tà irr tas Kungs fawu wahrdu apstiprinajs, ko winsch irr runnajs, jo es efmu zehlees manna Dahwi-da weetâ, un fehſchu us Jſraëla gohda-krehflu, itt kà tas Kungs irr runnajs, un efmu weenu nammu darrijs, ta Kunga ta Deewa Jſraëla wahrdam.
11. Un tur efmu es to fchkirstu lizzis, eekſch ka ta Kunga derriba irr, ko winsch ar Jſraëla behrneem darrijs.
12. Un winsch stahweja preekſch ta Kunga altari, wiffai Jſraëla draudſei pretti, un isplattija fawas rohkas.
13. Jo Salamans bij weenu warra krehflu darrijs, un starp teem pagal-meem lizzis peezas ohlektis garru, un peezas ohlektis plattu, un trihs ohlektis augstu, un winsch stahweja us to, un bij zelkôs mettees preekſch wiffas Jſ-raëla draudſes, un isplattija fawas roh-kas prett debbefi, un winsch fazzija:
14. Kungs, Jſraëla Deews, ne weens irr tahds Deews kà tu effi, debbefis un wirs semmes, kas tu to derribu un schehlastibu turri taweem kalpeem, kas no wiffas fawas firds tawa preekſchâ staiga.
15. Kas tu tawam kalpam mannam tehwam Dahwidam effi turrejs, ko tu winuam runnajs, jo ar tawu mutti effi tu runnajs, un ar tawu rohku effi tu o peepildijs, itt kà tas fchodeen irr.
16. Un nu, Kungs, Jſraëla Deews, turri tawam kalpam mannam tehwam Dahwidam, ko tu tam effi runnajs, faz-zidams: Tew ne truhks weens wihrs mannâ preekſchâ, kas us Jſraëla gohda-krehflu fehſch, ja tikkai tawi dehli fawu zellu farga, staigadami mannâ bauflibâ, itt kà tu mannâ preekſchâ effi stai-gajs.
17. Nu tad, ak Kungs, Jſraëla Deews! Laid jelle taws wahrds teefcham no-teek, ko tu tawam kalpam Dahwidam effi runnajs.
18. Jo pateefi, bij tam Kungam w semmes pee zilwekerm dſchwoht? Nà ta debbes un wiffu debbefchu debbe tew ne warr fanemt, zik masak tad fennams, ko es efmu taifijs? 1 Kehn. 8, Cſ. 66, 1.
19. Tapehz greeſees pee tawa ka- luhaſchanahm, un pee winna peeſa-fchanahm ak Kungs mans Deews! klaufi to faukſchanu un to luhaſchan ko taws kalps preekſch tawa wai-luhds.
20. Laid tawas azzis atwehru irr d-nâ un nakti pahr fcho nammu, p-e fcho weetu, no ka tu effi fazzijs: Ka wam wahrdam tur buhs eezeltam ta ka tu klaufi to luhaſchanu, ko taws ka-fchinî weetâ luhgs. 2 Mohs. gr. 20.
21. Klaufi tad to firds luhaſchanu wa kalpa un tawu lauſchu Jſraëla, tee ſchinnî weetâ luhgs, un tu kla no tawas mifchanas weetas no deb-fim, ak klaufi un peedohd.
22. Kad kas prett fawu tuwaku bi-apgrehkojees, un lizzis winnu ſwehr un nolahdtees, un ta ſwehreſcha nahk preekſch tawa altara ſchinnî na-mâ. 1 Kehn. 8, 31. 2 Mohs. gr. 22, 1.
23. Tad klaufi Tu no debbefim, darri, un teeſa tawus kalpus, un-greeſchahs, un tawam wahrdam fuhdſehs, un tawa preekſchâ ſchal na-mâ peeluhgs, un firſnigi peeſanuks. 5 Mohs. gr. 28, 25. 1 Kehn. 8, 33.
24. Kad arridſan tawi Jſraëla ſaul-eenaidneeku preekſchâ kauti tays, i tee pretti tew buhs grehkojufchi, un-greeſchahs, un tawam wahrdam fuhdſehs, un tawa preekſchâ ſchal na-mâ peeluhgs, un firſnigi peeſanuks. 5 Mohs. gr. 28, 25. 1 Kehn. 8, 33.
25. Tad klaufi Tu no debbefim, i peedohd tawu Jſraëla lauſchu grehku un wedd tohs atpakkał us to semmi-tu teem un winnu tehweem dewis.
26. Kad ta debbes buhs aisflehga, leetus ne buhs, tapehz ka tee prett te-buhs apgrehkojufchees, un tee ſchin-weetâ luhds, un tawam wahrdam goh-dohs, un no faweem grehkeem atarer-fees, kad tu tohs apbehdini. 1 Kehn. 8, 33.
27. Tad klaufi Tu debbefis, un pe-dohd tawu kalpu un tawu Jſraëla lau-ſchu grehkus, ka tu teem to labbu zeh-arahdi, kur teem jaſtaiga, un lertt-dohs.
