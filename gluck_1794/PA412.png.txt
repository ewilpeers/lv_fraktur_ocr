19. Tomehr Tew ne buhs to nammu pataifiht, bet taws dehls, kas no ta- weem gurneem nahks, Tas ustaifihs mannam wahrdam to nammu.
20. Tà irr tas Kungs ſawu wahrdu apſtiprinajs, ko winſch irr runnajs. Jo es efmu zehlees manna tehwa Dahwi- da krehflu, ittin kà tas Kungs runnajs, un es efmu to nammu pataifijs ta Kunga Iſraëla Deewa wahrdam.
21. Un efmu tur weenu weetu talfijs tam ſchkirſtam, eekſch ka ta Kunga der- riba irr, ko winſch ar muhfu tehweem darrijs, kad winſch tohs no Egiptes- ſemmes iswedde.
22. Un Salamans ſtahweja preekſch ta Kunga altari, wiſſai Iſraëla draudſei pretti, un isplattija fawas prett debbefim, un fazzija: 2 Laik. gr. 6, 12. 2 Matt. 9, 10.
23. Kungs Iſraëla Deews, ne weens irr tahds Deews kà tu, augſcham deb- befis, neds appakſchâ wirs ſemmes. Kas to derribu un ſchehlaſtibu turr ta- weem kalpeem, kas tawâ preekſchâ ar wiſſu fawu firdi ſtaiga.
24. Kas tu tawam kalpam mannam tehwam Dahwidam effi turrejs, ko tu tam effi runnajs, Tu effi ar tawn mut- ti runnajs, un ar tawu rohku to pee- pildijs, irr kà tas ſchodeen irr.
25. Un nu Kungs, Iſraëla Deews, turri tawam kalpam mannam tehwam Dahwidam, ko tu tam effi runnajs, fazzidams: Tew ne truhks weens wirs mannâ preekſchâ, kas us Iſraëla goh- da krehflu fehſch, ja tikkai tawi dehli fawu zellu farga, ſtaigadami mannâ preekſchâ, ittin kà tu mannâ preekſchâ effi ſtaigajs. 2 Sam. 7, 12. 16.
26. Nu tad, ak Iſraëla Deews! laid jelle taws wahrds teefcham noteek, ko tu tawam kalpam, mannam tehwam Dahwidam effi runnajs.
27. Jo pateefi, bij tam Kungam wirs ſemmes dſihwoht? Raugi, tahs deb- befis, un wiſſu debbefis tew ne warr fanemt, kà tad weh! ſchis nàms, ko es efmu taiſijs? Eſ. 66, 1. Ap. darb. 7, 48. nod. 17, 24.
28. Tapehz greeſees pee tawa kalpa luhgſchanhm, un pee winna peefauk- ſchanahm, ak Kungs! mans Deews! un klaufi to faukſchanu un to luhgſcha- nu, ko taws kalps ſchodeen preekſch- wu waigu luhdſ.
29. Laid tawas azzis atwehru ſta- deenâ un nakti pahr ſcho nammu, ve- ſcho weetu, no ka tu effi fazzijs: Mo- wahrds tur patt buhs; Ka tu klaufi luhgſchanu, ko taws kalps ſchinni w- tâ luhgs. 5 Mohſ. gr. 12, 11.
30. Klaufi tad to firds luhgſchanu wa kalpa, un tawu Iſraëla laufchu, tee ſchinni weetâ luhgs, un tu kla- tawâ miſchanas weetâ debbeſis, klaufi un peedohd!
31. Kad kas prett fawu tuwaku bu apgrechkojees, un lizzis winnu ſwehri un nolahdetees, un ſchi ſwehreſcha un lahſti preekſch tawu altari ſchir nammâ nahks,
32. Tad klaufi Tu debbeſis, un da un teeſa tawus kalpus, to netaiſnu teefadams, un dohdams winna zedu winna galwu, un to taiſnu dan dohdams winnu pehz fawas taiſnib.
33. Kad tawi Iſraëla laudis kauti ta preekſch faweem eenaidneekeem, k tee prett tew buhs grehkojuſchi, un tew atgreeſchahs, un tawam wahrda to fuhdſehs, un tew ſchinni namn peeluhgs, un firfnigi peefauks.
34. Tad klaufi Tu debbeſis, un pe dohd tawu Iſraëla ſauſchu grehkus, i wedd tohs atkal us to ſemmi, ko winnu tehweem effi dewis.
35. Kad ta debbes buhs aisflehgta, leetus ne buhs, tapchz ka tee prett te buhs apgrehkojſchees, un tee ſchinni weetâ luhgs, un tawam wahrdam fuhdſehs, un no faweem grehkeem at greeſifees, kad tu tohs apbehdini.
36. Tad klaufi Tu debbeſis, un pe dohd tawu kalpu un tawu Iſraëla ſau ſchu grehkus, ka tu teem to labbu zell parahdi, kur teem jaſtaiga, un leetu dohdi us to ſemmi, ko tu tawoem ſau dim par eemantoſchanu dewis.
37. Kad badda-laiks eekſch ſemmes buhs, kad buhs mehris, bulla-laiks, ruh- fa, fiſſeni, kukkaini, kad winnu eenaid neeks eekſch ſemmes prett winnu wah- teem lauſifees, jeb kahda zitta mohzib jeb fehrga buhs.
