19. Pehz to buhs tew to ohtru aunu nemt, un Aäronam, un winna dehleem buhs fawas rohkas us ta auna galwn likt.
20. Un tew buhs to aunu nokaut, un no winna affini nemt, un us Aärona labbas aufs-ſkripſtinu, un us winna dehlu labbas aufs-ſkripſtinu, tà patt us winna dehlu labbas rohkas ihkſchki, un us winnu labbas kahjas ihkſchki likt, un to affini buhs tew us to altari wifs apkahrt flazziht.
21. Tad buhs tew no ta affins nemt, kas us to altari irr, un no tahs fwaidiſchanas-eljes, un us Aäronu winna drehbehm flazziht, un us winna dehleem, un us winna dehlu drehbehm ar winnu, ka winſch fwehtihts effus, un winna drehbes, un winna dehlu drehbes ar winnu.
22. Un tew buhs no ta auna nemt tohs taukus, un to aſtri, un tohs taukus, kas tahs eekſchas apklaht, un ta auna taukus, un abbejas ihkſtis ar teem taukeem, kas pee tam irr, un to labbu plezzu, jo tas irr weens pildifchanas auns.
23. Un Weenu kummoſu maiſes, un Weenu karraſchu ar etzi ſajauktas maiſes, un Weenu raufchu no to neraudſetu maiſu kurwja, kam buhs preekſch ta Kunga waiga buht.
24. Un tew buhs to wiſſu likt us Aärona plaukſtehm, un us winna dehlu plaukſtehm, un to kuſtinaſchanas-uppuri preekſch ta Kunga, tas irr weens ugguns tam Kungam.
25. Pehz to nemm to no winnu rohkas, un eededſimi to us to altari, us to dedſamu-uppuri, par weenu faldu ſmarſcht preekſch ta Kunga, tas irr weens ugguns tam Kungam. 3 Mohs. gr. 8, 29.
26. Tad tew buhs ta pazelfchanas-uppura kruhtis no ta pluſtinaſchanas-uppura plezzu, kas pazelts un kuſtinahts irr, no ta pildifchanas auna, no to, kas Aäronam, un no ta, kas winna dehleem peedert.
27. Un tam buhs Aäronam un winna dehleem buht, par weenu muhſchigu eefahdiſchanu, no teem Iſraela behrneem, jo tas irr weens pazelfchanas-uppuris, un to Iſraela behrnu pazelfchanas-uppuram buhs pee winnu teikſchanas-uppureem buht: Winpazelfchanas-uppureem buhs preekſch Kunga buht. 3 Mohs. gr. 7, 31, 34. nod. 10, 14.
29. Tahs fwehtas drehbes, kas Aärona irr bijufchas, peederrehs winna dehleem pehz winnu, ka tee eekſch teefwaiditi tohp, un ka winnu rohka teeftahm pildita tohp.
30. Septinas deenas buhs tam teapwilkt, kas no winna dehleem winweetâ par preeſteri buhs, kam eekſch teefa-eeſchanas telts irr ja-eet, un ta fwehta jakalpo.
31. Tew buhs ta pildifchanas-uppura aunu nemt, un winnu gallu fweetâ wahriht.
32. Tad buhs Aäronam, un winna dehleem ta auna gallu ehſt, ar to maiſſi kurwî buhs, preekſch tahs fa-eeſchanas telts durwim. 3 Mohs. gr. 8, 31. nod. 10, 14. nod. 24, 9. Matt. 12, 4.
33. Un teem buhs tahs leetas ehtiko ta falihdſinaſchana irr notikkuſſiwinnu rohka pildita tohp, un to fwehtiti tohp, bet fweſchineekeem ne buhs ehſt, jo tahs irr fwehtoſriba.
34. Un ja kas atleek no ta pildiſchanasuppura gallas, jeb no fchihs maiſſiſrihtam, tad buhs tew to atlikkuſſiugguni fadedſinaht, tam ne buhs tapt, jo tas irr fwehts.
35. Tew buhs tad Aäronam, un winna dehleem tà darriht, pehz wiſſu, bettew eſmu pawehlejs, feptinas dardu buhs tew winnu rohkas pildiht.
36. Tew buhs arridſan ikdeenas nu wehrfenu par grehku-uppuri neeſpar tahm falihdſinaſchanahm, un tobuhs to altari ſchkihſtiht, to falihdſchanu darridams pahr winnu, un tobuhs winnu fwaidiht, ka winſch fwehtihts tohp.
37. Septinas deenas buhs tew falihdſinaſchanu darriht pahr to altari, un towinnu eefwehtiht, un tad buhs *altaris to fwehtu leetu fwehtiwifs, kas to altari aiskarr, tas tofwehtihts.
