Ta 19. Nodaſta.
No teem 6 gelbeſchanas pilofateem pre- teem, kas nerihſchu ohtru uokau, 1. 5. 9. buhs to tihſchu ſlepkaibu ſobdiht, 11. K- beſchu akmanus ne buhs ismebraht, 12. leezineeteem, 15. Un ka buhs wiltigus le- neekus ſobdiht, 16.
Kad tas Kungs taws Deews ti paganus isdeldehs, kurru ſem tas Kungs taws Deews tew dohs, tu tohs eemantoſi, un winnu pilſfat un winnu nammôs dſihwofi.
2. Tad buhs tew tribes pilſfatus atſcht tawas ſemmes widdû, ko tas Kung taws Deews tew dohs winnu eemanto 2 Mohſ. gr. 21, 13. 4 Mohſ. gr. 35, 9. Joſ. 20.
3. Taifi to zelku us turren, un to ti beſchu tawas ſemmes, ko tas Kung taws Deews liks eemantoht, trei- dakkâs buhs tew to ſchkirt, ka us ti ren behgt warr, ikkatrs, kas kahdu ti kawis.
4. Un to buhs darriht tam, kas wee- nokawis, kam us turren irr jahda, tas dſihwo, kas fawu tuwaku nerihſ nofittis irr, ko winſch pirmak ne eem deja. 2 Mohſ. 21, 13. 5 Mohſ. 4, 41. 4.
5. Tà, kad kas ar fawu tuwaku n ſchâ eetu malku zirſt, un winſch fan rohku ar zirwi pazeltu malku zirſt, ta dſelſe nomuktu no ta kahta, un a nemtu winna tuwaku, ka tas mir tas laid eekſch weena no ſcheem pil ſateem behgt, un dſihws paleek.
6. Ka tas affinu-atreebeis tam ne wejam ne dſihtohs pakkał, kad win firds irr ſakarfuſi, un tas panahk winnu, kad tas zelſch tahlu buhtu, nemtu winnam to dſihwibu, jebſchu kahda nahwes teefa pahr winnu gah- fi, jo winſch ne bij to pirmak eemihdeſ.
7. Tapehz pawehlu es tew, un fakk Tew buhs few trihs pilſfatus atſchku.
8. Un kad tas Kungs taws Deews tu wu rohbeſchu isplattihs, irtin kà win taweem tehweem ſwehrejs irr, un do tew wiſſu ſemmi, ko winſch runna irr taweem tehweem doht.
