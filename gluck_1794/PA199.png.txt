45. Un tahs Ahſera meitas wahrds bija Sahra.
46. Schee irr Ahſera behrnu ziltis pehz faweem ſkaititeem peezdefmits trihs tuhkſtoſchi, un tſchetrſimts.
47. Nawtalus dehli pehz fawahm zil-tim, no Jaëziëla irr to Jaëziëliteru zilts, no Gunus irr to Guniteru zilts.
48. No Jezera irr to Jezeriteru zilts, no Sillema irr to Silleliteru zilts.
49. Schee irr Nawtalus ziltis, pehz fa-wahm zilitim, un winnu ſkaititi bija tſchetrdefmits peez tuhkſtoſchi, ſeptinfimts.
50. Schee irr Iſraëla behrnu ſkaititi, ſefchreiſi fimts tuhkſtoſchi, weens tuhk-ſtohts, ſeptinfimts un trihsdefmits.
51. Un tas Kungs runnaja us Mohſu, un fazzija:
52. Scheem laid ta ſemme isdallita tohp par ihpaſchumu, pehz to wahrdu pulku.
53. Teem, kas daudſirr, buhs tew win-nu ihpaſchumu wairoht, un teem, kas maſak irr, buhs maſaki par ihpaſchu-mu doht, ikweenam pehz to pulku farou ſkaititu lai tohp tas ihpaſchums dohts.
54. Tomehr lai ta ſemme zaur fewifku meflofchanu isdallita tohp, pehz teem wahrdeem winnu tehwu ziltu buhs teem eemantocht. 4 Mohſ. gr. 33, 54. Joſ. 11, 23. nod. 14, 2.
55. Pehz tahs meflofchanas rahdiſcha-nu, laid ikweens ihpaſchums dallihts tohp, ſtarp daudſeem un maſeem.
56. Schee nu irr tee ſkaititi Lewiti pehz fawahm zilitim, no Gerſona irr to Gerſoniteru zilts, no Kaäta irr to Kaä-titeru zilts, no Merarus irr to Merari-teru zilts. 2 Mohſ. gr. 6, 16. 18. 19.
57. Schee irr Lewja zilts, to Libni-teru zilts, to Eebroniteru zilts, to Maë-literu zilts, to Muſiteru zilts, to Ko-raiteru zilts.
58. Un Kaäts dſemdinaſa Amramu, un Amrama fewas wahrds bija Joſe-bete, Lewja meita, kas Lewſam Egiptes-ſemmê dſimmufi, un fchi dſemdeja Am-ramam to Aaronu un to Mohſu, un Mirjami, winna mahfu.
59. Un Aaronam dſimme Nadabs un Abijus, Eeleazars un Jhtamars.
60. Bet Nadabs un Abijus bija no-mirru. N 4
