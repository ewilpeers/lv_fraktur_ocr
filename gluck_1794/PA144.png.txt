4. Jums buhs mannu teefu un man-
nu eeſtahdijumu darriht, un eekſch teem
ſtaigaht, Es efmu tas Kungs juhfu
Deews.

reht, un mannu teefu buhs jums dar-
riht, kurſch zilweks tohs darrihs, tas
zaur teem dſihwohs: Es efmu tas
Kungs. Ezek. 20, 11. 13. Reem. 10, 5.
Gal. 3, 12.
6. Ne weenam buhs pee jebkurra fa-
wa affins-radda pee-eet, winnas kau-
numu atfegt, es efmu tas Kungs.
7. Tawa tehwa kaunumu tew ne buhs atfegt,
ta irr tawa mahte, winnas kaunumu
tew ne ûuhs atfegt.
8. Tawa tehwa feewas kaunumu tew
ne buhs atfegt, tas irr tawa tehwa
kaunums. 3 Mohſ. 20, 11. 1 Kor. 5, 1.
9. Tawas mahfas, tawa tehwa mei-
tas, jeb tawas mahtes meitas kaunu-
mu, jeb kas mahjâ jeb ahrâ dſimmufi,
tew ne buhs atfegt.
10. Tawa dehla meitas, jeb tawas
meitas kaunumu tew ne buhs
atfegt, jo tee irr taws kaunums.
11. Tawa tehwa feewas meitas kau-
numu, kas tawam tehwam dſimmufi,
(ta irr tawa mahſa) tew ne buhs atfegt.
3 Mohſ. 20, 17.
12. Tawa tehwa mahſas kaunumu tew
ne buhs atfegt, ta irr tawa af-
fins-radneeze. 3 Mohſ. 20, 19.
13. Tawas mahtes mahſas kaunumu
tew ne buhs atfegt, jo ta irr tawas
mahtes affins-radneeze.
14. Tawa tehwa-brahła kaunumu tew
ne buhs atfegt, winnas feewu tew ne
buhs aiskahrt, ta irr tawa tehwa
la feewa. 3 Mohſ. 20, 20. Ezer. 15.
15. Tawas weddekles kaunumu
buhs atfegt, ta irr tawa dehla
tew ne buhs winnas kaunumu atfegt.
3 Mohſ. 20, 12.
16. Tawa brahła feewas kaunumu
ne buhs atfegt, tas irr tawa kaunums,
3 Mohſ. gr. 20, 21.
17. Kahdas feewas, un winnas
tas kaunumu tew ne buhs atfegt,
winnas dehla meitu, neds winnas
tas meitu buhs tew nemt, winnas
numu atfegt, tahs irr affins-tawu
ta irr weena beskauniga leeta.
3 Mohſ. gr. 20, 14.
