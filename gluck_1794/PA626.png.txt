Ta 13. Dſeefma.
Lubgſchana eekſch noſtumſchanas nu ſirds bailehm.
1. Weena Dahwida dſeefma preekſch ta augſta dſeedataja.
2. Zik ilgi ak Kungs! tu mannis itt wiſ fai aismirfifi? Zik ilgi gribbi tu tawu waiqu preekſch mannim apfleht?
3. Zik ilgi buhs man padohmu mekleht fawa dwehſele, un fawa firdi ik deenas bailotees? Zik ilgi pa-augſtina fees mans eenaidneeks pahr mannim?
4. Skattajs paklaufi mannis ak Kungs! mans Deews, avgaifmo man nas azzis, ka es nahwe ne aismeegu.
5. Ka mans eenaidneeks ne fakka: Es efnu winnu pahrwarrejs, un manni prettineeki vreezajahs, ka es ſchaubohs.
6. Bet Es zerreju us tawu ſchehla ſtibu, manna firds lihgſmojahs eekſch tawas peſtifchanas.
Ta 14. Dſeefma.
Mahziba no tahs zilwezigas dabbas famaita ſchanas nu glahbſchanas.
1. Weena Dahwida (dſeefma) preekſch ta augſta dſeedataja.
Tee gekki fakka fawa firdi: Deen newaid, tee irr famaitajſchees fagahnijſchees ar fawu darbu, ne weenewaid, kas labbi darra. Dahw. dſ. 53, Reem. 3, 11. 12.
2. Tas Kungs ſtattahs no debbes teem zilweku behrneem, raudſiht, at jelle kas ſaprattigs buhtu, kas Deenmekletu.
3. Bet tee irr wiſſi atkahpuſchee wiſs notaſ irr ſmirdofchi tappuſchi, n weens irr kas labbi darra, irr ne Ween weenigs.
4. Woi tad teem launadarritajeem irr nekahda atſihfchana, kas mann laudis ehd, (itt) ka tee maiſi ehſtu, to Kungu tee ne peefauz. Mik. 3, 2.
5. Tur tee ar bihjafchanu bihſtahs, Deews irr pee to taiſnu radda. Iſab. 15.
6. Juhs neewajat ta nabbaga pademu, ka tas Kungs irr winna patwerun.
7. Ak! kaut ta Peſtifchana no Zinas pahr Iſraelu nahktu, zaur to tas Kungs fawus zeetuma laud atkal atweſtu, tad preeza fees Je kabs un Iſraels lihgſmoſees.
Ta 15. Dſeefma.
Kas tizzigeem klahjabs nu ne klahjabs.
1. Weena Dahwida dſeefma.
Kungs, kas mahjohs tawa dſi wokli? un kas dſihwohs us tan ſwehtu kalnu?
2. Kas beswainigi ſtaiga, un to taiſi budſenn, un runna to pateeſibu no ſuli dubbina. Eſa. 35, 15. Eweſ. 4, 25.
3. Kas ar fawu mehli ne aprunna, f wam tuwakam launa ne darra, un kana wallodu prett fawu tuwaku ne zetlijs, jepſchu few par ſkahdi, tatſchwinſch to ne pahrlauſa.
