19. Es preſauzu mannis mihletajus, bet tee irr manni wihluſchi manni preeſteri un wezzajee irr pilſſatâ to dwehſeli islaidufchi, jo tee mekleja preekſch ſeſimbarribu, ka tee warretu fawudwehſeli atwirdſinaht.
20. Uſraugi Kungs, jo man irr bail, mannas eekſchas irr fajukkuſchas, manna finds lezz mannâ widdû, jo es efnungchi prettiturrejſces, ahrâ irr tas ſohkins man tohs behrnus aplaupijs, un eekſchâ ta nahwe.
21. Tee gann dſird manni waidam, bet eepreezinataja man newaid, wiſſi manni eenaideecki dſird mannu nelaimibu, un preezajabs, ka Tu tà eſſi darrijs, kad tu to deenu atweddifi, ko tu to eſſi isfauzis, tad tee buhs itt kà es.
22. Lai wiſſa winnu blehdiba tawâ eekſchâ nahk, un darri teem, ka tu man eſſi darrijs wiſſu mannu pahrkahoſchanidehl, jo mannas waideſchanas irr dauds, un manna firds irr nogurrufi.
Ta 2. Nodalla.
Baimann dſeeſma pahr to galleju pohſtikana tahs Zianas meitas.
Fà irr tas Kungs to Zianas meitu ar fawu dufmibu itt kà (ar padebheſſu) ziklahjis? Winſch irr to Jſraela gohida no debbes us to ſemmi nomettis, winſch fawu kahju pameſlu ne irr atmmejs fawas dufmibas deenâ.
3. Tas Kungs irr wiſſus Jehkaba dſihkelus aprihjs, un ne irr faudſeis, winſch irr tahs ſtipras weetas tahs meitas Juda nolauſis eekſch fawas dufmibas, winſch irr darrijs, ka tee pee ſemmes gull, winſch irr fagahnijs to wallibu, un winnas leel-kungus.
4. Winſch irr eekſch fawas bahrgas dufmibas wiſſu raggu Jſraela falauſis, winſch irr fawu labbu atwilis, kad tee eenaideecki nahze, winſch irr prett Jehkabu apſkaitees kà ugguns leefma, kas wiſsapkahrt aprihj.
5. Tas Kungs irr tappis kà eenaideecks, un fawu labbu rohku pezeblis kà prettineeks, un nekawis wiſſu, kas ozzim patihk, winſch irr fawu bahrſibu eekſch tahs Zianas meitas dſihwokia isaghſis kà uggumi.
6. Winſch irr fawu dſihwokli noylehfis, kas tà biſa, kà dahrs, un fawu faeefchanas weetu maitajs, tas Kungs irr lizzis eekſch Zianas ſwehtkus un ſwehdeenas aismirſt, un eekſch fawas bahrgas dufmibas to kehninu un preeſteri atmettis.
7. Tas Kungs irr fawu altari atmettis, winſch irr fawu ſwehtu weetu isnihzinajs, tohs muhrus winnas ſkaiſtu nannu irr eekſch aismirſt, un eekſch fawas bahrgas dufmibas to kehninu un preeſteri atmettis.
8. Tas Kungs irr dohmajs to muhri tahs Zianas meitos ſamaitaht, winſch irr to mehra auklu (pahr to) wilzis, fawu rohku winſch ne irr atgreeſis, kamehr winſch to aprihtu, un winſch irr to preekſch muhri ar to muhri kohpâ apbehdinajs, tee irr wahſi tappufchi.

9. Winnas wahrti irr ſennem grimmufchi, winſch irr winnas aisſchauſamus maitajs un fatreezis, winnas kehninſch, un winnas leel-kungi irr ſtarp teem paganeem bes bauflibas, ir winnas praweefchi ne atrohd wairs parahdiſchanu no ta Kunga.
10. Tee wezzajee tahs Zianas meitas fehſch pee ſemmes, un zeeſch kluſſu, tee mett pihſchlus us fawu galwu, irr maſfus apjohſuchi, tahs jumprawas no Jeruſalemes nokarr fawu galwu peeſemmes.
11. Mannas azzis irr no affarahm isehſtas, mannas eekſchas irr fajukkuſchas, mannas aknis irr pee ſemmes iskrattitas, tahs fatreekſchanas dehl tahs meitas mannu kauſchu, tadehl ka tee behrnini un ſihdami us pilſſata eelahm pamirſt. Raud. dſ. 1, 20.
12. Kad tee us fawahm mahtehm ſakka: Kur irr labbiba un wihns? Kad tee us pilſſata eelahm pamirſt kà nokauti, kad tee fawas dwehfeles islaiſch fawu mahtu klehpi.
13. Kahdu leezibu warru es tewi atneſt? Ar ko warru es tewi lihdſinaht, tu Jeruſalemes meita? Ar ko warru es tewi lihdſinaht, ka es tewi eepreeznaju, ak jumprawa, tu Zianas meita! Jo tawa fatreekſchana irr tik leela kà ta juhxa, kas warr tewi dſeedinaht?
