15. Neggi tahs atraitnes affatas peht to waiqu ſemmē notekk? tatſchu nē nu faukſchana eet us augſchchu pretti, kas tahs isſpeech.
16. Kas Deewam labpraht kalpe, tas tays peenemts, un winna luhgſchana uskahpyj lihds debbeſim.
17. Ta luhgſchana weena paſem-miga ſpeeſchahs zaur tahm padeht beſim, un ne mittahs, pirms ta preekſchā nabk, neds norimſt, ne kams tas wiſſu-augſtakais uska-ko, un tas pats neſſihs taiſnu teeh, un turrehs to ſohdibu.
18. Tas Kungs arridſan ne kawefins un ne buhs lehnprahtias pahr teem, pirms tas teem neſchehligeem tohs un-nus fatreeks, un makfahs teem pree-neem ar atreebſchanu, kamehr winſch to neewataju draudſi isdeld, un to n-ka-ſa taifnu ſepterus ſadausa.
19. Teekams winſch tam zilwelana makfahs pehz faweem darbeem, un dehs teem zilwekeem to algu pehz winnudeh mahm, kamehr winſch fawu tau-ka teeſu isteeſa, un eepreezehs tohs it fawu apſchehloſchanu.
