141. Es eſmu mafs un neewahs, bet tawas pawehleſchanas es ne aismirſtu.
142. Tawa taiſniba irr muhſchigi taiſniba, un tawa baufliba irr pateefiba.
143. Behdas un bailiba irr man usgahjufi, bet tawi baufli irr mannas eelihgſmoſchanas.
144. Tawu leezibu taiſniba irr muhſchiga, leez man to ſapraſt, tad es dſihwoſchu.
145. Es peefauzu no wiſſas firds, paſklaufi mann Kungs, tad es tawus likkumis turreſchu.

147. Es nahku pirms ne ka patti rihkrahſla, un kleedſu: Us tawu wahrdu zerreju es.
148. Mannas azzis pamohſtahs pirms, ne ka tahs nakts waktis, no taweem wahrdeem pahrdohmaht.
149. Klaufi mannu balfi pehz tawas ſchehlaſtibas, Kungs, darri mann dſihwu pehz tawas teefas.
150. Manni wiltigi wajataji us mann lauſchahs, tee atkahpj tahlu no tawas bauflibas.
151. Kungs, Tu eſſi tuwu, un wiſſi tawi baufli irr pateefiba.
152. No eefahkuma ſinnu es, ka tu tawas leezibas muhſchigi eſſi dibbinajs.
153. Usluhko mannas behdas, un isglahb manni, jo tawu baufliba es ne aismirſtu.
154. Iſteefa tu mannu teeſu, un atpeſti mannis, darri mann dſihwu pehz taweem wahrdeem.
155. Ta peſtſchana paleek tahlu no teem besdeewigeem, jo tee tawus likkumus ne mekle.
156. Kungs, daudſ irr tawu apſchthloſchanu, darri mann dſihwu pehz tawahm teeſahm.
157. Daudſ irr mannu wajataju un prettineeku, bet no tawahm leezibahmes ne atkahpju.
158. Es redſu tohs pahrkahpejus, un man fabp ka tee tawus wahrkus ne tumb.
159. Usluhko, ka es tawas pawehleſchanas mihleju, ak Kungs! darri manndſihwu pehz tawas ſchehlaſtibas.
160. Taws wahrds wiſſas notal irr pateefiba, un wiſſas tawas taiſnibas tewas paleek muhſchigi. Jahn. 17, 17.
161. Tee leeli kungi waja mannis bet wainas, bet manna firds bihſtahpreekſch taweem wahrdeem.

167. Manna dwehſele turr tawas leezibas, un es mihleju tahs lohti.
168. Es pafargu tawas pawehleſchanas un tawas leezibas, jo wiſſi manna preekſch tawa naht, darrimann gudru pehz tawu wahrdu.
169. Ak Kungs! lai manna kleegſchamann gudru pehz tawu wahrdu.
170. Lai manna firds luhgſchamann gudru pehz tawa wahrdu.
171. Mannas luhpas teiktu * teiks tawu teizamu flawu, kad tu tawus likkumus man buhſi mahzijs.

172. Manna mehle runnabs no tawu teizamu wahrdeem, jo wiſſi tawu baufliirr taifniba.
