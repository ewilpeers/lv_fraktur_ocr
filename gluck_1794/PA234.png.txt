Ta 15. Nodalta.
To ſeptitu gaddu buhs allaſchin par ſwab-badibas-gaddu turreht, 1. Ta paſcha teeſas un likkumi, 2. Ne weenam deedelneckam buhs Iſraela ſtarpâ buht, 4. Kà tohs nabbagus buhs apgahdaht, 7. Tohs tizzibas brahſus, kad tee ſew pahrdewuſchees buhs ſeptita gad-dâ atlaiſt, 11. Wiſſu pirmdſimtibu buhs tam Kungam ſwehtiht, 19.
Ap ſeptitu gaddu buhs tew weenu at-laiſchanu turreht. 2Moh. 21, 2. 3 Moh. 25, 8. 10. Jer. 34, 14.
2. Schis nu irr tahs atlaifchanas darbs, ka ſkweenam aisdewejam, kas fawam tuwakam ko labb aisdewis irr, buhs at-laiſt, tam ne buhs to no fawa tuwaka, jeb no fawa brahła atpraffiht, tapehz ka tam Kungam weena atlaifchana isfaukta tappuſi.
3. No ta ſweſchineeka buhs tew at-praffiht, bet kas pee tawa brahła irr, to tam atlaid.
4. Bes ween, ja winſch nabbags tawâ ſtarpâ ne irr: Jo tas Kungs ſweh-tidams tew ſwehtihs tai ſemmè, ko tas Kungs taws Deews par ihpaſchumu tew dohs winnu eemantoht.
5. Ja tu tikkai ta Kunga tawa Dee-wa balſi klaufiſt, turredams wiſſus ſchohs bauflus, ko es tew ſchodeen pa-wehlu.
6. Jo tas Kungs taws Deews tew ſwehtihs itt kà winſch tew runnajs irr, ad aisdohſi tu daudſ tautahm, bet tu ne aisdemſi us atdohſchanu, un tu wal-difi vahr daudſ tautahm, bet winnahm ne buhs pahr tew waldiht. 5Moh. 28, 12. 13.
7. Kad tawâ ſtarpâ weens nabbags, jeb kahds no taweem brahłeem buhs, jeb kahdôs tawôs wahrtôs tawâ ſemmè, ko tas Kungs taws Deews tew dohs, tad tew ne buhs fawu firdi apzeetinaht, neds tawu rohku preekſch tawu nabba-gu brahli aisfleht. 3Moh. gr. 25, 35.
8. Bet atwehrdams atwerr winnan tawu rohku, un aisdohd winnam ais-dohdams, zeek gann irr preekſch winne truhtuma, kas winnam truhkſt.
9. Sargajs, ka tawâ firdi kahds Be-liala darbs ne eſſus, ka tu fazzitu: Ta-ſeptihts gads, tas atlaifchanas gads it tuwu, un tawa azz buhtu launu pret tawa brahli, to nabbagu, ka tu win-nam ne dohd, tad brehks winſch pah-tew us to Kungu, tad tas buhs ten par grehku.
10. Dohd winnam labpraht, laid ta wa firds launa ne irr, kad tu winnan dohd, jo ſchihs leetas dehl ſwehtihs ten tas Kungs taws Deews wiſſa tawi darbâ, un eekſch wiſſa, pee ka tu tawi-rohku isſteepi. Matt. 5, 42. Luhk. 6, 35.
11. Jo tur ne truhks nabbagi tai ſem-mè, tadehl pawehlu es tew, un faklu Atwehrdams atwerr tawam brahlan tawu rohku, kas ſpaidihts tohp, un nabbags irr tawâ ſemmè. Matt. 26, 11 Jahn. 12, 8.
12. Kad taws brahls weens Ebreëris jeb weena Ebreëru feewa tew pahrdoh ta buhs, tad buhs winnam tew ſefchu-gaddus kalpoht, bet ſeptita gaddâ buh tew winnu ſwabbadu no tew atlaiſt. 2Moh. gr. 21, 2. 3Moh. gr. 25, 39. 40. Jer. 34, 1.
13. Un kad tu winnu ſwabbadu atlai-tad ne buhs tew winnu tukſchu atlaiſi.
14. Apdahwinadams apdahwini wir-nu no tawa gannama pulka, no tawa-klehts, un no tawa wihna-ſpaida, ar t-tas Kungs taws Deews tew ſwehtij irr, no ta buhs tew winnam doht.
15. Un peeminni, ka tu kalps eſſi biji-Egiptes ſemmè, un ka tas Kungs taw-Deews tew irr atpeſtijs, tapehz pawel-łu es tew ſchodeen tahs leetas.
16. Bet ja winſch us tew fazzihs: E-ne gribbu no tew iſeet, tapehz ka es te-un tawu nammu mihłoju, ka tam y-tew labbi klahjahs.
17. Tad nemm ihlenu, un urb eekſ-winna aufi, pee tahm durwim, t-buhs tam tew par kalpu buht muhſchar-tà patt buhs tew tawai kalponei do-riht. 2Moh. gr. 21, 6.
