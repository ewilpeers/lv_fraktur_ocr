7. Bet tew, un taweem dehleem ar tew, buhs juhſu preeſterammatu far- gaht wiſſâs altara leetâs, un pee ta, kas eekſchpuffè, un ais to preekſchwelkamu irr, pee ka jums darbotees, juhſu preeſterammatu dehſchu es jums kà weenu (ſawadi) dahwinatu kalpoſcha- nu; Un tam ſweſchineekam, kas pee- eet, buhs mirt.
8. Un tas Kungs runnaja us Aäro- nu; Un Es, raugi, es efmu tew to fargafchanu mannu pazelfchanas-up- puru dewis, ar wiſſahm to Iſraëla behru ſwehtahm leetahm efmu es tohs tew dewis, tahs ſwaidifchanas labbad, un taweem dehleem par weenu likkumu muhſcham.
9. Tas laid tew peederr no tahm wifs- ſwehtakahm leetahm, no teem ugu- nim wiſſi winnu uppuxi ar wiſſeem winnu ehdama-uppuxeem, un ar wiſſeem winnu noſeedſibas-uppuxeem, ko tee man dohd, tas laid tew, un ta- weem dehleem, weena wifsſwehtaka leeta irr.
10. Wiſsſwehtakâ weeta buhs tew to ehſt, wiſſi no wihra-kahrtas laid to ehd, tam buhs tew ſwehtahm buht. 3 Mohſ. gr. 6, 16.
11. Un tas laid tew peederr, tas pa- zelfchanas-uppuris, ko tee dahwina ar wiſſeem Iſraëla kuſtinafchanas-uppu- reem, es efmu tew tohs dewis, un ta- weem dehleem, un tawahm meitahm ar tew, par weenu likkumu muhſcham, ikkatrs, kas tawa namma ſchkihſts irr, lai to ehd. 3 Mohſ. gr. 10, 14. nod. 22, 11.
12. To wiſſulabbaku no eljes, un to wiſſulabbaku no wihna, un no labbibas winnu primajus, ko teem buhs Kun- gam doht, efmu es dewis.
