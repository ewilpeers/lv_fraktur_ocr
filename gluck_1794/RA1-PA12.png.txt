12 (Nod. 10. n.) Juhdites (Nod. 11.) 19. Un tee brihnijahs pahr winnas ſkaiſtumu, un brihnijahs winnas pehz aridſan pahr teem Iſraëla behrneem, un fazzija zits us zittu: Kas warretu ſchohs łaudis ſmahdeht, kam tahdas ſkaiſtas ſeewas irr? ne peederrehs nekahdu no winnu wihreem atlizzinaht, kad tee palaiſti łauchtu, tad tee warretu wiffu pafauli peewilt. 20. Pehz isgahje Olowernus waktineeki un wiffi winna fullaini, un eewedde winnu telti. 21. Un Olowernus duffeja us fawugultu ais tahm gardinehm, kas no purpura un ſelta un ſmaragdeem un dahrgeem akmineem bija ſa-austas. 22. Un kad tee winnam no tahs fazzija: tad isgahje winſch eekſch tahs telts preekſch iſtabas, un fudraba lukturi tappe winnam preekſch neſti. 23. Kad nu Juhdite no winna un winna fullaineem redſeta łaua, tad brihnijahs wiffi pahr winnas ſkaiſtumu: Un ta mettehs us fawu waiqu, un peeluhdſe winnu: Bet winna kalpi uszehle to. Ta 11. Nodalla. Un Olowernus fazzija us to: Turrees labbu prahtu keewina, ne bihſtees tawa firdi, jo es wehl ne weenam zilwekam eſmu łauna darrijs, kas apnehmees irr Nebukadnezaram tam kehninam wiffas ſemmes kalpoht, 2. Buhtu arri tawi łaudis, kas ſchinnis kalnu-weetâs dſihwo, manni ne mizinajſcht, es ne buhtu prett winneemmannu ſchkehpu pazehlis, bet tee irr pafchi few to eedruppinajſchi. 3. Nu tad, fakki man: Kapehz tu nowinneem behguſi un pee mums nahkuſi? jo tas tew par labbu buhu, ka tu effi atnahkufi. Turrees labbu prahtu, tu palikſi ſcho nakti un jo prohjam dſihwa. 4. Jo ne weenam buhs tew łaunu betlabbu darriht, itt ka teem kalpeem manna kunga ta kehnina Nebukadnezara noteek. 5. Tad fazzija Juhdite us wintzu: Peenemn tohs wahrdus tawas kalpones, un lai tawa meita tawa preekſchâtunna, jo es ne ſtahſtifchu mellus mannam kungam ſchinni nakti. 6. Jo ja tu tawas kalpones wahrdeemklaufifi, tad Deews to leetu ar tewim pilnigi padarrihs, un manna kunga padohmi welti ne isdohfees. 7. Jo tik teefcham ka tas kehninawiffas ſemmes Nebukadnezars dſihwaun wiffa winna warra, kas tewi ſtatijs irr wiffas dwehfeles ihſteni nowadiht, ka zaur tew neween zilweki tawnam kalpo, bet arridſan tee ſwehri lanka, un lehpi, un tee putni appakſch debefim zaur tawu ſpehku dſihwo appakſch Nebukadnezara un wiffa winnanamma. 8. Jo mehs effam tawu Gudribu dſidejſchi, un tohs gudrus padohmins was dwehfeles, un wiffai ſchat ſemniir fazzihts, ka tu ween eekſch wiffakehnina walſtibas tas labbatajs effi warrenas faprafchanas, un brihnikigs eekſch gudrahm karra leetahm. 9. Un nu, tahs wallodas pehi, ſakiors tawa runnâs runnajs irr, ſefam mehs winna wahrdus dſirdejſchiJo tee wihri eekſch Betulijas irr wunu peenehmufchi, un winſch irr teemſtahſtijs wiffu, ko winſch tawa pafchakahds ſpehbinſprett winneem ko ceſpedja tee prett fawu Deewu ne irr grebkojſchi. 10. Tadeht kungs un walditajs, ſan nizzini winna wallodu, bet palurri tawa firdi, jo ta irr pateefa, jo peemuhfu tautas ne warr atreebtees, un tokahds ſohbins apkaitinahs, zikkahrt tee ſcho negantſhapadarrihs. 11. Un nu, ka manna kunga padohmisundarbs nedarrihts ne paleek, (12. f.)ka winneem ta nahwe jaw preekſchazim irr, jo weena apgrehkoſchana irtohs ſanehmufi, ar ko tee fawu Deewapkaitinahs, zikkahrt tee ſcho negantſhapadarrihs. 12. Jo tapehz ka teem barribas truhkums irr un leels uhdens dads, tad intee apnehmufchees fawus lohpus kauun wiffu, ko winneem Deews ſankbauftôs irr aisleedſis, ka tee to ne barditu, to irr tee apnehmufchees eht. 13. Jr tohs pirmajus no labbibas untohs deſmitus no wihna un eljes, katee paglabbajſchi par ſwehtitam leetahm preekſch teem preeſtereem, kasJeruſalemê mufu Deews preekſchâſtabw, gribb tee arridſan apchſt, kur tomehr neweenam no teem łaudim wadaiir ne ar rohkahm to pafchu aiskahet. 14.
