6. Tai deenâ eezelfchu es tohs wirfneekus Juda kà weenu deggoſchu ugguns-kurri ſtarp to malku, un par weemu deggoſchu lahpu ſtarp teem kuhliſcheem, un tee aprihs pa labbas un kreiſas puffes wiſſas tautas apkahrt, un Jeruſaleme paliks wehl fawâ weetâ eekſch Jeruſalemes.
7. Un tas Kungs ispeſtihs to dſihwokli Juda kà pirmak, ka ta augſtiba ta namma Dahwida, un ta gehdiba to eedſihwotaju no Jeruſalemes ne leelahs prett Judu.
8. Tai deenâ pafargahs tas Kungs tohs eedſihwotajus no Jeruſalemes, un kas winnu ſtarpa wahjſch buhs, tas buhs taî deenâ kà Dahwids, un Dahwida nams buhs kà Deewa, kà ta Kunga engelis winnu preekſchâ.
9. Un taî deenâ mekleſchu es tohs paganus nomaitaht, kas prett Jeruſalemziellahs.
10. Bet pahr Dabwida nammu, un pahr Jeruſalemes eedſihwotajeem isleefchu es to Garru tahs ſchehlaſtibas un lubgſchanas. Un tee us manni raudſifees, ko tee irr zaurduhruſchi. Un tee ſchehlofees pahr winnu ar tahdu ſchehlofchanu, kà pahr weenu weenigu dehlu, un tee waiddehs gauſchi pahr winnu, kà kas waid pahr weenu pirmdſintu. Joël. 2, 28. Iabn. 19, 37. Jer. 6, 26. Amos. 8, 10.
11. Tai deenâ buhs eekſch Jeruſalemes leela raudafchana, kà ta raudafchana Adad Rimmona eekſch tahs eleijas Megidus. 2 Keh. 23, 29. 30. 2 Laik. gr. 35, 22.
12. Un ta ſemme ſchehlofees, ikweena zilts fewiſchki, ta zilts ta Dahwida namma fewiſchki, un winnu fewas fewiſchki. Ta zilts ta Nahtana namma fewiſchki, un winnu fewas fewiſchki.
13. Ta zilts Simejus fewiſchki, un winnu fewas fewiſchki.
14. Wiſſas zittas ziltis, ikweena fewiſchki, un winnu fewas fewiſchki.
Ta 13. Nodalla.
No Deewa ne-isſmeſſama ſchehlaſtibas-awota.
Tai deenâ buhs weens Awots atwehrtis preekſch Dahwida namma, un preekſch Jeruſalemes eedſihwotajeem prett teem grehkeem, un prett to neſchkihſtibu.
2. Un taî deenâ, ſakka tas Kungs Zebaot: Isdeldeſchu es no tahs ſemmes te deewekſu wahrdus, ka tee wairs naptaps peeminneti, ir tohs prawerſchus un to neſchkihſtu garru gribbu es no tahſemmes atuemt. Oſe. 2, 17.
3. Un tas notiks, ja kas wairs prawerſchu mahzibu fluddina, tad winnu tehws, un winna mahte, kas winnu irr dſemdinajſchi, us to fazzihs: Tew ne buhs dſihwoht, tapehz ka tu wiltibu effi tunnajs eekſch ta Kunga wahrda, un winna tehws, un winna mahte, kas to utdſemdinajſchi, winnu nodurs, kad winnu praweeſchu mahzibu fluddina.
4. Un tas notiks taî deenâ, tad taps tepraweeſchi kaunâ, ikweens fawas prarahdiſchanas deh, kad winni prawerſchu mahzibu fluddina, un ne apwitswairs weenu ſpalwainu mehteli mellidami.
5. Bet winnam buhs fazziht: Es neefmu praweets, es efmu weens wihts, kas to ſemmi kohpj, jo weens zilweis inmanni faderrejs no mannas jaunibas.
6. Un ja kas us winnu fazzihs: Kas tahs tahdas wahtis tawâs rohkâs? Tad buhs winnam fazziht: Ta efmu es ſakults mannu mihłotaju namma.
7. Sohbins! usmoſtees prett mannu Gannu, un prett to wihru, kas mansBeedris irr, fakka tas Kungs Zedeci: Sitt to Gannu, tad iskaififers tas gannams pulks, bet es greeſiſchu fawurthku pee teem fihkeem. Matt. 26, 21.
8. Un tas notiks wiſſâ taî ſemme, ſakka tas Kungs: Diwi dallas taps eekſchtahs isdeldetas, un islaidihs to garru, un ta trefcha dalla taps eekſch tahs allizinata.
9. Un es weddiſchu to trefchu dallasugunk, un ſchkihſtifchu tohs kà ſadrabs tohp ſchkihſtihts, un pahrbauſſchu tohs, kà ſelts tohp pahrbauſſhts. Tas peefauks mannu wahrdu, un Espaklaufiſchu to, un fazziſchu: Tew intmanni ſaudis, un Tas fazzihs: TasKungs irr mans Deews. Ef. 48, 10. 1 Petr. 1, 17.
