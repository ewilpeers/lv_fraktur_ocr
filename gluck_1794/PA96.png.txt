28. Tahs zeſſamas kahrtis buhs tew no Sittim-kohka taifiht, un tohs ar ſeltu pahrwilkt, un tam galdam buhs pee tahm neſtam tapt.
29. Tew buhs arridſan taifiht winna *Blohdas, un **Kaxxotes, †kannas, un ††kaufus, ar ko tam buhs apklahtam tapt, no tihra ſelta buhs tew darriht.
*Blohdas, ka tahs preekſchleekamas maiſes eelſch tahm warretu likt, ſeb tahs nogſihneht.
**Kaxxotes, ar ko to wihraku warretu is- nemt, kad tas us to kwehpinaſchanas-al- tari eededſnuahrs tappe.
†Kannas, ſeb bikkerus, kur to flazzifcha- nas affini warretu eelikt, un isleet pee ta ehdamu-nupura.
††Kaufus, ar ko tik labbi to wihraku, kas us tahm preekſchleekamahm maiſehm biſa 3 Mohſ. gr. 24, 7. ka arri tahs preekſchleeka- mas maiſes, un to wihraku warretu apklaht.
30. Un tew buhs allaſchin us to galdu tahs preekſchleekamas maiſes, preekſch mannu waigu likt. 2 Mohſ. 37, 17.
31. Tew buhs arridſan weenu lukturi taifiht, no tihra ſelta, no zeeta ſelta buhs tam lukturam taifitam tapt, win- na kahtam un ſarreem, winna kaufem, winna pohgahm, un winna pukkehm buhs no ta isſteeptees. 2 Mohſ. 37, 17.
32. Tà ka feſchi ſarri no winna fah- neem isſteepjahs, trihs ſarri no ta luktur- ra weenas puſſes, un trihs ſarri no ta luktura ohtras puſſes.
33. Weenâ ſarrâ buhs trihs kaufini, itt ka mandelu-reekſti, weena pohga, un weena pukke buht, un ohtrâ ſarrâ buhs atkal trihs kaufini, itt ka mandelu-reek- ſti, weena pohga, un weena pukke buht, tà buhs teem ſefcheem ſarreem, kas no ta luktura isſteepjahs.
34. Bet pee pafcha luktura buhs trim kaufem buht, ar fawahm pohgahm, un ar fawahm pukkehm.
35. Un tur buhs weenai pohgai ap- pakſch diwi ſarreem buht, (un) no teem (isſteeptees), un wehl weenai pohgai ap- pakſch diwi ſarreem, (un) no teem (is- ſteeptees), tà buhs tam buht ar ſefcheem ſarreem, kas no ta luktura isſteepjahs.
36. Winnu pohgahm, un winnu ſar- reem buhs no ta isſteeptees, tam wiſſam buhs zeetahm darbam no tihra ſelta buht.
37. Tew buhs winnam arridſan fepti- nus eljes-lukturus taifiht, un winna eljes-lukturus augſchâ zelt, ka tee win- nam pretti ſpihd.
38. Winna luktſchkehrem, un dſi- fameem rihkeem buhs no tihra ſelt buht.
39. No weena Talenta tihra ſelta buht taifiht, ar wiſſeem ſcheem rihkem 2 Mohſ. gr. 38, 24. 25.
40. Luhko tad, kad tu to darri pe- winnu preekſch-ſihmi, kas tew kah- rahdita irr.
Ta 26. Nodalla.
Ta Deewa dſihwokla taifihums no deſ- gardinehm un trihs dekkeem, 1. 7. 14. Ee un dſihwokla kliuki, 15. Paſils preekſch- kamais ſtarp tahs ſwehtas un wiſo-ſwehtas weetas, 31. Paſils auts pafchas dſihwok- durwis, 36.
Un to dſihwokli buhs tew taifiht un deſmits gardinehm, no faſchke- natahm dſihjahm, un paſillum, un purpuxa, un dahrga farkanuma, un Kerubim, ar padohmu buhs tew ta- taifiht. 2 Mohſ. gr. 36, 8.
2. Weenas gardines garrums, un diwideſmits un aſtonas ohlektis irr, un weenas gardines plattums, laid tſch- tras ohlektis irr, wiſſahm gardinu buhs weenâ mehrâ buht.
3. Peezahm gardinehm buhs weenai pee ohtras faliktahm buht, atkal buh- peezahm gardinehm weenai pee ohtras faliktahm buht.
4. Un tew buhs paſillas zilpas taifiht pee weenas gardines wihles, appatſch gallâ, pur ta falikta tohp, tà patt buh- tew arridſan darriht pee tahs wehli- tahs pehdigas gardines, kur ta ohtrâ reiſē falikta tohp.
5. Peezdefmits zilpas buhs tew pee weenas gardines taifiht, un peeza- mits zilpas buhs tew pee gardines ſtud reem taifiht, kur tahs ohtrâ reiſē ſtud liktas tohp. Schahm zilpahm buhs weenai to ohtru fanemt.
6. Tew buhs arridſan peeza- defmits ſtud ta kahfifchus taifiht, un tahs gardines weenu pee ohtras ar ſcheem kahfifcheem buhs tew to taifiht. 2 Mohſ. gr. 36, 14.
